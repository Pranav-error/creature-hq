Questions

What is Creature HQ?

Creature HQ is a free, self-paced roadmap for learning fullstack web development, from how the browser works to deploying a real app. You raise a creature companion as you go: passing checkpoint quizzes trains its stats, and shipping projects levels it up until it evolves.

Who is it for?

Beginners who can already think a little like a programmer and want a structured path through HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, SQL, authentication, TypeScript, testing and deployment, without collecting endless courses.

Does it cost anything?

No. Creature HQ is free and has no ads. You only need an account so your progress is saved.

Where do I learn each topic?

Anywhere you like. Every checkpoint names a topic and links to YouTube, Wikipedia and web searches for it. Then you prove it with a short five-question quiz.

How do projects work?

Project checkpoints need real proof: a public GitHub repository and a deployed link anyone can open, plus a short write-up of what you built and learned. Only projects give XP, and XP is what makes your companion evolve.

Is my progress saved across devices?

Yes. Sign in with Google or email and your companion, quests, quiz answers, notes and projects are saved to your account and load on any device.
